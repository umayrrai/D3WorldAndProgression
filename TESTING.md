# Manual test script

For a person, or an agent driving a browser. Run `deno task dev` and open the local URL with `?seed=121`. Before each section, reload. To read logical state, use `game.state` in the console. To read what has played, use the panel beside the grid, including the `#live` text, or look at the marker.

**Promotion rule:** a bug found by hand or by an agent becomes a permanent test in `core/` before it is fixed, unless it truly lives in `game/`, in which case add a step here.

| #  | Action                                                                                         | Expected observation                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | Load the page                                                                                  | One canvas. A pale marker at the center of a dark map. No console errors.                                                                                                                            |
| 2  | Tap the right arrow                                                                            | The marker slides one cell east. `#live` says `moved to 1, 0`. `player` is `{ x: 1, y: 0 }`.                                                                                                         |
| 3  | Walk until a river or spikes cell is ahead, then press toward it                               | The marker lurches and flashes red, then returns to its cell. `#live` says `blocked by river at ...` or `blocked by spikes at ...`. `player` has not changed.                                        |
| 4  | Tap an arrow during a walk                                                                     | The tap waits. The marker finishes the walk, then takes that step. It never skips a cell.                                                                                                            |
| 5  | Mash arrows quickly, then stop                                                                 | The marker settles on the cell `player` reports.                                                                                                                                                     |
| 6  | Hold an arrow down                                                                             | The marker keeps walking. Holding into a wall repeats the bump.                                                                                                                                      |
| 7  | Load `?seed=7`                                                                                 | A different map.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 8  | Run `game.state` in the console                                                                | Plain data: `seed` , `player` , `inventory`. No terrain list.                                                                                                                                        |
| 9  | Press an arrow several times                                                                   | Position and Terrain in the panel change as each step plays, and never ahead of the marker.                                                                                                          |
| 10 | Make the window short, then press the down arrow                                               | The page does not scroll. The marker moves.                                                                                                                                                          |
| 11 | Click the sound button, then press an arrow                                                    | The button reads `Sound: off`, and the arrow still moves the marker.                                                                                                                                 |
| 12 | Click the sound button again                                                                   | The button reads `Sound: on`, and you hear a two-note chime.                                                                                                                                         |
| 13 | Tab to the sound button and press Space                                                        | It toggles. Arrow keys still move the marker.                                                                                                                                                        |
| 14 | Listen while walking and bumping (headphones or speakers)                                      | A soft tick on each step, and a low buzz on each bump. **This is the only check that a person can hear them.**                                                                                       |
| 15 | On a high-resolution display, or at browser zoom 200%                                          | Grid edges stay sharp. The marker stays centered. Run `document.querySelector("canvas").width`: it is the canvas `clientWidth` times `devicePixelRatio`.                                             |
| 16 | With sound on, press G on a fresh trees or bones tile.                                         | The marker flashes white and pulses, the gather sound plays, and the tile turns barren. The item count in the inventory beside the map goes up as the pulse starts.                                  |
| 17 | Press G again without moving off the tile.                                                     | The message says “nothing left to gather here,” and the inventory count stays the same.                                                                                                              |
| 18 | Tap an arrow to walk onto a fresh resource tile, then tap G while the marker is still sliding. | The inventory stays the same during the walk. Once the marker arrives, the gather pulse starts and the item count goes up.                                                                           |
| 19 | Stand next to a river or spikes cell, on walkable ground, and press G                          | Gathering reads the cell you stand on, never the one you face. Nothing is taken from the neighbor.                                                                                                   |
| 20 | Load the page and read the Recipes list                                                        | Three lines, numbered 1 to 3: boat, bone axe, torch, each with what it needs. The torch line says it needs the bone axe.                                                                             |
| 21 | Press 1 with an empty inventory                                                                | `#live` says `cannot make a boat: need 2 wood, 1 bone more`. The inventory does not change, and a low buzz plays.                                                                                    |
| 22 | Gather 2 wood and 1 bone, then press 1                                                         | The marker pulses, a two-note craft sound plays, `#live` says `made a boat`, and the inventory shows `boat: 1` with the wood and bone gone.                                                          |
| 23 | Press 3 while holding wood and tar but no bone axe                                             | `#live` names the bone axe as missing, and nothing is used up.                                                                                                                                       |
| 24 | Craft a bone axe, gather wood and tar, then press 3                                            | A torch is made, and the bone axe is still in the inventory. (Tar comes from a river cell, which you cannot stand on until step 4: in the console, `game.state.inventory.tar = 1` stands in for it.) |
| 25 | Tap a number key while the marker is mid-walk                                                  | The craft waits until the walk ends, and the inventory changes only then.                                                                                                                            |
| 26 | Press 1 several times quickly with enough items for one boat                                   | One boat is made. Later presses fail and say what is missing.                                                                                                                                        |
