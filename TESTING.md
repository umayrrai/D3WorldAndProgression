# Manual test script

For a person, or an agent driving a browser. Run `deno task dev` and open the local URL with `?seed=121`. Before each section, reload. To read logical state, use `game.state` in the console. To read what has played, use the panel beside the grid, including the `#live` text, or look at the marker.

**Promotion rule:** a bug found by hand or by an agent becomes a permanent test in `core/` before it is fixed, unless it truly lives in `game/`, in which case add a step here.

## Recipe readiness and focus

1. Reload with seed 121. Tab to Bone axe and press Enter. The button shows the cost and `Missing 6 bone`, stays enabled and focused, and `#live` explains why the craft failed.
2. Keep Bone axe focused and walk to and gather from six fresh bone tiles with the arrow keys and G. During the last gather pulse, the button still says `Missing 1 bone`. Once input and playback settle, it says `Ready` and keeps focus.
3. Press Enter. During the craft pulse, the button still says `Ready`. After playback, the same focused button says `Already owned`. Press Enter again and verify that `#live` says `you already have a bone axe`.
4. Repeat the final gather from a fresh run, but press 1 during that gather pulse. The button keeps its previous shortage through both pulses, then updates directly to `Already owned`.
